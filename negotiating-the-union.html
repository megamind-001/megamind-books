The problem

You've been sold a myth

You were told marriage completes you. That singlehood is a waiting room. That love alone will carry you through. Every one of these ideas is either false or dangerously incomplete — and they are quietly wrecking modern relationships.

  • The completion myth — that a spouse fills what only you can fill
  • The number obsession — treating a partner's past as a score instead of a pattern to understand
  • The silent-provider myth — that financial strain equals a man's spiritual failure
  • The similarity trap — assuming two people alike will automatically stay agreed

If you've felt behind at 30, 40, or 50 for still being single — argued yourself hoarse trying to win at home — or discovered something after the wedding you wish you'd known before — this book was written for you.
